14. TEV (Time-Equity-Value) Framework

TEV governs how contributions are valued and how equity/ownership is allocated within the K0RE ecosystem.

14.1 Core Formula

Total Contribution Score (TCS) = (T x 0.3) + (E x 0.2) + (V x 0.5)

  • T (Time): Hours contributed
  • E (Energy): Effort/impact score (1-10, weighted by engagement, impact, initiative, collaboration, consistency)
  • V (Value): Financial/resource contribution

14.2 Equity Rate Calculation

Time-Equity Rate = Average(Market Hourly Rate, Current Rate) + Startup Risk Premium (15-20%). 38% equity pool allocated to skilled resources (co-founder pool). Share issuance at: year-end OR $3M CAD retained earnings OR seed funding round.

14.3 TEV Cannot Override Ethics or Sovereignty

TEV is subordinate to the constitutional framework. No contribution calculation may: commodify human identity, force participation, create coercive incentive structures, or bypass consent requirements.
